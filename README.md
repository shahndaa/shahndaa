### About Me

I'm an **AI/ML Engineer** and Computer Science graduate specializing in building **production-grade AI systems** — from healthcare AI and demand forecasting to computer vision and OCR pipelines. I care about solving real problems with rigorous engineering, not just notebooks that never ship.

🌊 I believe in bringing **balance** to messy data — every pipeline is a little bit of water (flow), fire (speed), earth (structure), and air (creativity).

- Top 20 Winner, **EVA Group Limited (EVAI) Hackathon**
- National 4th Place | Project Lead, **Digitopia Competition (MCIT)**
- Currently exploring: LLM fine-tuning, RAG systems, and AI agent workflows
